Components I'd include
1. Dataset selector
- Which uploaded document/data source is being analyzed
- File name/type
- Change dataset button
2. Analysis types
Cards/tabs such as:
- Overview — general business performance
- Trends — revenue, sales, customers over time
- Products — best/worst performing products
- Customers — customer behaviour/segments
- Financial — revenue, expenses, profit, margins
- Custom Analysis — eventually let the user specify what they want
3. KPI cards
Depending on the dataset:
- Revenue
- Orders
- Customers
- Profit
- Growth
- Average Order Value
These should be dynamic, not hardcoded demo numbers.
4. Visual analysis area
This is the main part of the page:
- Line charts
- Bar charts
- Pie/donut charts where appropriate
- Comparison charts
- Tables
5. AI-generated insights
A dedicated section like:
Key Insights

DOCIQ explains what the numbers actually mean in simple business language.
Example:
Revenue increased 18% in Q3, primarily driven by higher sales in Category A.

6. Recommendations
Separate insights from actions:
Recommended Actions
• Increase inventory for Category A
• Review declining sales in Category C
• Investigate customers with reduced purchase frequency
